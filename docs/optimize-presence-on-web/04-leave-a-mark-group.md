## Key takeaways

- ✓ **What they do exceptionally well:**
  - **Named blue-chip client logos** — single biggest credibility move
  - Per-regulation service pages (each = its own SEO landing)
  - SKI Framework approval = unlocks DK public-sector buyers
  - Visible ISO certifications (not just claimed in JSON-LD)
  - Strong DA-first content
- ✗ **What they're not:** developer-led. They are a *consultancy* — they don't ship code, build MCP servers, or write LangChain integrations. Their delivery model is advisory + audits + training, not engineering.
- **Implications for cocode.dk:**
  1. **Add a client-logo bar.** Even 3–5 logos (with permission) destroys the "anonymous freelancer" perception faster than any other change.
  2. **Pursue SKI Framework eligibility** if going after DK public sector. Not trivial — requires CVR, references, financial data — but transformative if achieved.
  3. **Add visible ISO/cert badges** even when the cert is "knowsAbout"-level (familiarity) rather than "I am ISO 27001 certified" (which Babak as an individual would not be). *Be specific about claim level* — "trained in ISO 27001 implementation" is honest and still SEO-positive.
  4. **The differentiator versus Leave a Mark:** they advise; cocode.dk *builds*. Lead with "I write the code that implements your compliance — most consultants leave at the audit." That position is wide open.
