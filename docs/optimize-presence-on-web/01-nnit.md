## Key takeaways

- ✓ **What they do well:** corporate scale signals, structured per-vertical service pages, productized AI offering (Alera AI), enterprise SEO via PR distribution
- ✗ **What they do poorly / miss:** they explicitly *don't* mention NIS2/DORA/EU AI Act as keywords on the homepage — their AI positioning is industry-vertical (life sciences) rather than regulation-keyword. **Opportunity for cocode.dk:** owning the regulation-named keywords (`NIS2 implementation Denmark`, `DORA compliance developer`, `EU AI Act consulting Copenhagen`) which NNIT competes for only weakly via vertical pages.
- **Insight for solo positioning:** NNIT is uncatchable on brand and scale, but they leave the *long-tail regulation-named SME keywords* wide open. A solo consultant who explicitly indexes `/eu-ai-act-compliance-sme/` etc. can intercept that traffic.
